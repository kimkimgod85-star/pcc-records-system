PCC Records – Pagadian Capitol Colleges Registrar Online Request

The official records management system of Pagadian Capitol Colleges. Students and alumni can request school records such as the Transcript of Records (TOR), Certificate of Enrollment, Diploma copy, and Good Moral Certificate online, pay through GCash or the cashier, schedule a pickup date, and track their request status from the Registrar's Office.

Office of the Registrar · Rizal Avenue, Tuburan District, Pagadian City · Mon–Sat 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM
